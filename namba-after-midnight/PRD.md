===========================
VISON
===========================

Namba station Osaka. I just missed last train. Need to find a place to have fun. It's Autumn in Kansai. I've been invited by friends. Real ramen is sooo good; fulfilling refreshing. The night is late but young under the neon lights. Sun will be up soon...must make the early train back to Kawachinagano. The early 90s. Tatsuro Yamashita-san was a hit. Natsukashii!.

===========================
USERS
===========================

1). Late-night commuters who are either enjoying the nightlife or stayed late for work.
2). Bilingual users - who can speak a little Kansai-ben

===========================
FEATURES
===========================

- Must-have(s): Hero image, Scroll back to top button/icon, sticky navigation, footer; accessibility toggle in Ui - this is should have icons for contrast and text. contrast controls for sight-impaired users, and text controls weight and size of the fonts. 

- Should-have(s): Train itinerary, language toggle controls (English/Japanese)- this controls the text whether it is in English or Japanese. Default language is English. This could later be determined based on IP - in Japanese = Japanese, outside of Japan = English.

- Nice to have(s): Some visual animations of falling stars, and ramen; imagery is color vector line-art; Music juke-box built into UI - will play at least 3 Tatsuro Yamashita's hits from the 90s. With volume, and sound on and off controls.

===========================
DESIGN STANDARD
===========================

Texture: flat design, parallax scrolling, spacious, easy-to-read, inviting

Color scheme: 
	dark: #100F26
	red: #BF2A2A
	light: #F2E4C9
	orange: #F2A950
	pink: #F23D91
	white: #ffffff
	Other: It is okay to define and use adjacent/complementary colors for accessibility, support, and/or contrast.


Typography: Japanese google fonts "Noto Sans Japanese"; English google fonts "Tilt Neon"

===========================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
===========================

- Targeted at least for Late-night commuters who are either enjoying the nightlife or stayed late for work.

- Must-have(s) and Should-have(s) criteria are met

- Design standard is met with correct typography in user-selected language and color palette.
